Where Winds Meet

A free open-world wuxia action RPG developed by Everstone Studio and published by NetEase.

About the Game

Where Winds Meet takes you back to the turbulent Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period of ancient China. You start as a wandering swordsman whose fate is tied to a mysterious jade pendant. Travel across mountains, river towns, temples and ancient ruins, learn martial arts from different sects, and decide whether you become a righteous hero or a reckless outlaw.

The game focuses on player freedom. Your actions change your reputation, trigger different quests and even attract bounty hunters if you break the law.
