Wuthering Waves

An open-world wuxia action game set in a mysterious ancient land.

About the Game

Wuthering Waves brings a fresh wuxia experience. Players travel across mountains, rivers and hidden towns, master powerful martial arts, uncover old secrets and fight fierce enemies. The world is full of dynamic weather, vertical exploration and fluid combat.

Every choice shapes your journey. You can be a wandering swordsman, a hidden hermit, or a challenger against powerful evil forces.
