Where Winds Meet

An open-world wuxia role-playing game set in the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms.

About the Game

Where Winds Meet is a sandbox wuxia game produced by Everstone Studio and published by NetEase. Players take the role of a wandering swordsman travelling across ancient China. You can explore vast landscapes, learn martial arts from different schools, interact with hundreds of NPCs and make choices that shape your reputation.

There is no single fixed ending. You can become a righteous hero protecting civilians, or a fugitive wanted by authorities. Every decision brings new chances and new dangers in this turbulent wuxia world.
