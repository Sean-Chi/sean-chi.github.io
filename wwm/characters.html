Characters

The Wanderer / Protagonist

You are the wanderer from Qinghe Village. Your destiny is tied to a strange jade pendant. You do not belong to any fixed sect at first. Your choices decide how other people treat you.

Allies and Rivals

You will meet skilled swordsmen, travelling doctors, sect masters, imperial spies and veteran generals. Some of them seek justice, while others chase power and hidden secrets. Each important NPC has their own goals and hidden stories.

Enemy Types

Bandit leaders, corrupted fighters, shadow assassins and powerful sect bosses. Most boss fights focus on timing, parry and movement instead of simple attacks.
