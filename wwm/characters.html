Characters

Traveler / Protagonist

You are a wanderer without fixed sect. You learn martial arts from different masters, make friends and enemies along the road. Your decisions change how NPCs treat you.

Allies and Rivals

You will meet swordsmen, healers, rogue fighters and powerful sect leaders. Some want justice, some chase power, and some hide dark secrets. Many characters have their own personal stories and hidden goals.

Enemy Types

Corrupted fighters, wild beasts, ghosts and evil cultivators. Boss fights often test your movement, timing and strategy instead of simple attack spamming.
