Gameplay Features

Fluid Wuxia Combat

Combat depends on parry, dodge and counterattack. You can use swords, spears, blades, fans and umbrellas. Learn special kung fu skills and switch fighting styles freely during battle.

Open World Exploration

Climb cliffs, ride horses, swim and glide across the land. Look for hidden martial manuals, secret tombs and side quests all over the map.

Reputation & Crime System

Help villagers to gain good fame, or rob and hurt civilians. Criminal actions will create a bounty, and hunters will come after you. You may even be captured and put into prison.

Life Skills

Players can cook, brew medicine, play music and craft items. These life activities make the ancient wuxia world feel alive.
