Gameplay Features

Fluid Wuxia Combat

Fast sword combos, parry, dodge and aerial moves. Timing is very important. You can switch fighting styles and use special ultimate skills.

Open World Exploration

Climb cliffs, glide across valleys, swim through rivers. Secret chests, hidden quests and ancient scrolls are scattered everywhere.

Quest & Reputation System

Help villagers, join or oppose sects. Your reputation changes NPC dialogue and unlocks special missions and rewards.

Martial Arts Collection

Find and learn different kung fu manuals. Mix skills to build your own fighting style.
