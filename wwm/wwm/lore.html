Lore & World

The Background

Long ago, great disasters broke the old order. Ancient powers fell, and many hidden forces woke up again. Legends of swords, curses and forgotten sects spread across the land.

Regions

  • Misty mountain valleys where hidden clans live
  • Ancient ruined cities filled with traps
  • River towns with peaceful daily life and secret plots
  • Dangerous dark forests controlled by monsters and evil practitioners

The world mixes quiet natural scenery and dangerous fighting zones. You can find peaceful villages, then step into deadly ruins minutes later.
