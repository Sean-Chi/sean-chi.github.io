Lore & World

The Background

The story takes place during the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms, a chaotic period of war and separation. Old dynasties collapsed, and many martial sects, secret forces and local warlords fight for power. A mysterious relic brings hidden danger to the whole land.

Regions

  • Qinghe Village: the quiet hometown of the player
  • Kaifeng: the crowded capital full of political secrets
  • Misty mountains and ancient academies
  • River towns, bandit camps and hidden temples

The world has day-night cycles and changing weather. You can join daily life in towns, watch performances, trade goods, or sneak into dangerous forbidden areas.
