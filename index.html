Explore an Infinite Procedural Galaxy

No Man’s Sky is a sci-fi exploration and survival game developed by Hello Games. Every star you see in the sky is a sun, orbited by unique planets waiting to be discovered. You can fly seamlessly from planetary surface into deep space, with no loading screens between worlds. This universe contains more than 18 quintillion planets, all generated by procedural algorithms.

Your journey begins stranded on an alien world with a broken starship. From here, you choose your own path: become an explorer, a trader, a fighter, or a base builder. There is no fixed single way to play.
