Must-Try Dim Sum

  • Har Gow: Crystal shrimp dumplings, the symbol of dim sum.
  • Siu Mai: Open-topped pork and shrimp dumplings.
  • Egg Tart: Sweet baked custard tart with flaky crust.
  • Char Siu Bao: Soft steamed buns filled with sweet barbecue pork.
  • Cheong Fun: Smooth rice noodle rolls.
