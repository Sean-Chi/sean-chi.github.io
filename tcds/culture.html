Yum Cha: Tea and Dim Sum

Yum Cha means "drink tea". People go to teahouses in the morning or weekend to chat, relax and share small plates. Popular tea choices are Pu-erh, Oolong and Chrysanthemum tea. Tea helps balance the rich taste of dim sum.

It is a social tradition. Many families keep this habit for generations.
